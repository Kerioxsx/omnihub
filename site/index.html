    Your phone, as a remote

    Scan a QR code.
    That's the whole setup.

    The PC serves the phone app itself — nothing to install from a store, nothing in the cloud. Pair once with a QR code or PIN, over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted.

    • Files both ways — browse, download with resume, send photos to the PC, get files from it.
    • Music — cover art, controls, volume and bass, lyrics in time.
    • Games — start a game on the PC with its boost, from the couch.
    • Minecraft servers — start them, see who's on and where, their inventory, the live map and the console; let a friend in.
    • Volume & calls — every app's volume, mute your mic in every app, Mute mic and Deafen for Discord, WhatsApp or Nyxen calls.
    • Open apps — close any program on the PC, or quit the ones that hide in the tray.
    • Power — lock, sleep or shut down, with a countdown you can cancel.
    • Screen — watch the PC live; mirror an iPhone or Android to the PC.

    New in 0.8.0 · My PC

    Your PC, in 3D

    OmniHub draws your PC from what Windows already knows: processor, graphics card, each memory stick, motherboard, cooler and fan hubs. Nothing is installed to read them. Pick a lighting profile and the 3D PC lights up with it while the real one does, through OpenRGB, for ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, Corsair, NZXT, Lian Li and more. Fans run Silent, Default (Auto) or Fast, with safety limits that never let a quiet profile cook a part. A screen inside the case, or an NZXT Kraken's display, shows your picture, GIF, video, live stats or the song playing.

    New in 0.11.0 · Chats

    Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code and Codex, in one place

    Every conversation you have on your PC is on one list: chats with Claude and ChatGPT in OmniHub, and the conversations Claude Code and Codex keep in your projects. Open one and keep going: the answer streams in with every step it takes, and Claude Code and Codex work in the project's own folder with your own sign-in, doing only as much as you allow. Start a new one anywhere. Do it all from your phone too, like texting. Save any answer as an idea in one tap, and see what you've used: Claude Code's 5-hour window and tokens, Codex's plan limits, and what your API keys spent. Your keys stay encrypted on your PC.

    Improved in 0.13.4 · Mods

    Mods for every game, and Claude makes new ones

    OmniHub reads your Steam library and works out how each game takes mods. For the 300+ games on Thunderstore, you search and install in one click, with the mods each one needs and the game's mod loader. A zip, a 7z or a folder you downloaded goes where the game looks for it. Every mod switches off and comes out cleanly, because OmniHub keeps a copy of every game file it replaces. Balatro gets Lovely and Steamodded, GTA V Enhanced gets Script Hook V .NET for Story Mode, and Unreal games get .pak mods and UE4SS. For Minecraft, it finds the modpacks you already have in Prism Launcher, CurseForge and the Modrinth App, with their mods and worlds, so you can mix one, host it (or a world you played alone) as a server, or share it with friends in one file with the cover picture and your server in their list. Mixing Minecraft modpacks puts every mod in once, at the version made for the Minecraft you choose, and gives the mix a cover blended from its packs' icons, or painted from them by AI with your own Pollinations account. With your own Anthropic API key, Claude reads a game, writes the mod you describe and hands it over for one-click install. Online competitive games are left alone, so no account gets banned.

    New in 0.12.0 · Servers

    Your own Minecraft server, running 24/7 on your PC

    Pick vanilla, Paper for plugins, or Fabric, Quilt, Forge or NeoForge for mods, and OmniHub downloads the server and the Java it needs, checks every file, and keeps it going: it starts with your PC, comes back if it crashes, backs up the world and stops cleanly when you shut down. Make one from a Modrinth modpack or your own mix, and only the mods a server needs go in. See who's on and exactly where, open their inventory to give or take things, teleport, heal or kick, manage the whitelist and how many can play, and watch everyone move on a live map OmniHub draws from the world itself. Add mods, plugins and data packs from Modrinth, or have Claude make a data pack or a Paper plugin for you. Let friends in over the internet with the router port opened for you. And if some of your friends don't have a paid Minecraft account, let everyone in: OmniHub keeps your operators safe, so nobody can join with your name and take over. All of it works from your phone too.

    Improved in 0.15.0 · Lights

    Your Govee lights, in sync

    OmniHub finds every Govee light on your network, even behind routers that hide them, and drives it live. The lights behind your screen take its colours from where they sit, up to 30 times a second, bright and true, and eased so nothing flickers. Lights that can't take a colour per segment get one colour, the main colour of their part of the screen rather than a pale mix, so every light follows, and your keyboard and PC lighting can follow them too. Put music on in any app and they pulse on the beat in the album cover's own colours, gliding from one colour to the next, show the sound from bass to treble, or flow with the song. Add effects and one-click presets, wake up to a sunrise and fall asleep to a fade-out. With your Govee API key, each light's own scenes, music modes and settings are there too, down to a speaker's volume on lights that have one.

    Aurora

    Your song becomes the show

    The song's official music video fills your screen — matched to the song by ear, even when the video opens with a story, up to 4K. Playing on Spotify? Its Canvas or video can be mirrored right behind the lyrics. No video? The album's animated cover, or its whole cover, sharp on any monitor. Over it, short lyrics in the cover's own colours, an emoji in the same colour at least every other line — in English and Spanish — and soft light in the corners. Want your own look? Give it any font and any emoji pack — Twemoji, Noto, Discord emotes, a game's item textures or an emoji font — and the words get the matching pictures. Pick a cover and press play.

    A sketch in your browser. In OmniHub, Cinema plays the artist's own music video when there is one (muted — your music app stays the sound — in YouTube's player, kept in time with the song), else their animated cover, else the cover at its original size. The lyrics follow the song word by word, and the light follows the real sound (Windows' loopback, never the microphone).

    Game boost

    Press Play. OmniHub gets out of the way.

    A profile per game — Fortnite, VALORANT, CS2, Apex, Overwatch 2, Roblox, Minecraft and more, found on your PC. Competitive sets the game itself for the most frames (Fortnite's NVIDIA Reflex On + Boost, Nanite and Lumen off; Roblox's graphics slider at its lowest), and its NVIDIA driver profile too; Quality leaves its graphics alone, so a 4K Ultra game stays 4K Ultra. Either way OmniHub clears Windows out of the way, measures every frame, and puts everything back when you quit. New in 0.15: a one-click Fortnite Max FPS setup, a rendering-mode choice and a cache cleaner; Roblox shaders like RoShade and Bloxshade with the official ReShade; every Roblox flag to pick from, each one switched on or off. And OmniHub itself sleeps while you play.

        Questions
        
      Good to know
      
      
      
      
      
        
      Is OmniHub free?
      Yes — free and open source, with no ads, no account and no paid tier.
      
        
      Does it send my data anywhere?
      No. There is no OmniHub server. The only internet requests are optional: song lyrics from LRCLIB, sharper covers from Apple's iTunes catalogue, music videos from YouTube (only the song's title and artist are searched), the password breach check (only the first 5 characters of a hash), update checks on GitHub, and pings you start yourself.
      
        
      Why does Windows warn when I install it?
      The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen hasn't seen it before. Choose More info → Run anyway. Updates after that install from inside the app.
      
        
      Does the phone part work with iPhone and Android?
      Both. The phone app runs in the browser (Safari, Chrome…) and can be added to the home screen. iPhones can also mirror their screen to the PC with AirPlay.
      
        
      Is the game boost safe with anti-cheat?
      It only changes Windows settings — power plan, notifications, priority, GPU choice, Wi-Fi — and never touches a game's memory or files. Roblox flags go in Roblox's own settings file.
      
        
      My phone says "site can't be reached".
      Update to 0.2.1 or later — it fixes a Windows bug that refused phones on Wi-Fi. Then open Phone → Connection check: it tests the address from the PC and fixes the firewall in one click.
