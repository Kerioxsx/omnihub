Get OmniHub.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. It keeps itself up to date after that.

  1. Download the installer. It's a single .exe file.
  2. Run it. No admin rights needed. Windows doesn't recognise new apps yet, so choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Pair your phone. In OmniHub, open Phone, choose Pair a phone, and scan the QR code.

Questions

Is OmniHub free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads, no account, no purchases and no paid version. The only thing that can cost money is optional: if you add your own Claude or OpenAI key, those companies bill you for what you use, and OmniHub shows your usage.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. There's no OmniHub server, and nothing is sent to its maker. Some features talk to the service they're about, such as lyrics sites for the song that's playing or GitHub for updates. The privacy policy lists every service and exactly what it receives.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen hasn't seen it before. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Updates after that install from inside the app.

Does the phone part work on iPhone and Android?

Both. The phone app runs in the browser and can be added to your home screen. An iPhone can also show its screen on the PC over AirPlay.

Is the game boost safe with anti-cheat?

The boost changes Windows settings and the game's own settings files, never a game's memory or code, which is what anti-cheat looks for. No one outside a game's company can promise how its anti-cheat behaves, though, and mods are a different matter: check a game's rules before modding an online game.

How do I delete everything OmniHub saved?

Everything is on your PC. Uninstall OmniHub in Windows Settings, then delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\OmniHub folder. The privacy policy lists the other folders it may have saved files in, and how to clear parts of it from inside the app.

Who makes OmniHub?

Kerioxsx, an independent developer, not a company. The code, releases and issue tracker are on GitHub; open an issue to get in touch.

My phone says the site can't be reached.

Make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi, then open Phone, Connection check in OmniHub. It tests the address from the PC and fixes the Windows firewall in one click.
