Licence

OmniHub is released under the MIT licence. You may use it, copy it, change it and share it, for free, as long as the licence notice stays with it. The helper tools OmniHub downloads keep their own licences, which come with them.

No warranty

OmniHub is provided as it is, without any warranty. It changes real things on your PC: it deletes and shreds files when you ask, changes Windows and game settings, and installs mods. Read what a button says before you press it, and keep backups of anything you can't lose. Shredded files can't be recovered by anyone, including you.

To the extent the law allows, the maker of OmniHub isn't liable for damage or lost data that comes from using it.

Using it fairly

  • Use the phone remote, screen sharing and file access only on devices you own or have permission to use.
  • Download only what you have the right to download. Sites like YouTube have their own rules about saving videos.
  • Mods and game settings can conflict with a game's rules, especially in online games with anti-cheat. Check a game's rules before changing it; you're responsible for your accounts.
  • When you host a Minecraft server, Mojang's EULA applies, and OmniHub asks you to accept it first.

Other services

Some features use other companies' services, such as YouTube, Spotify, Govee, Anthropic, OpenAI, Steam, Thunderstore and Modrinth. Their own terms apply when you use them, and OmniHub can't promise they'll keep working. The privacy policy lists what each one receives.

Fees

OmniHub costs nothing. There are no purchases, subscriptions, ads or paid versions, and none are hidden anywhere in the app.

One thing can cost money, and only if you set it up: if you add your own Claude or OpenAI API key, those companies bill you directly for what you use. OmniHub shows your usage so you can keep an eye on it.

Refunds

Since OmniHub is free, there's nothing to refund. If anyone charged you for OmniHub, it didn't come from its maker: ask them for your money back, and please tell us where you found it. The real download is only at the GitHub releases page linked on this site.

Who makes OmniHub

OmniHub is made by Kerioxsx, an independent developer, not a company. The source code, releases and issue tracker are at github.com/Kerioxsx/omnihub. To get in touch, open an issue.
