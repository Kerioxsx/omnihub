100% free on DaFont). It is used for headings in the Minecraft theme. It is
the author's work and not covered by OmniHub's MIT licence.

OmniHub is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft. The Minecraft theme's
blocks, clouds and sky are drawn by OmniHub's own code; item pictures come
only from a pack the user chooses on their own PC and are never shipped.

Condensed by The Barlow Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(`BarlowCondensed-OFL.txt`). Used for headings in the Bleach themes.

The Bleach themes (Bankai, Hollow, Mugetsu) are fan-made looks. OmniHub is
not affiliated with Tite Kubo, Shueisha, VIZ Media or Studio Pierrot. Their
crescents, ink strokes, stripes and flames are drawn by OmniHub's own code;
