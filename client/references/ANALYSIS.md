# Video-by-video visual breakdown

These are observations, not claims about source code or exact runtime capabilities. Phone-recorded footage, compression, video editing, shaders, scripted presentations and server-side features can all be mixed together. If an animation is not clearly linked to a confirmed kill, treat it as **visual inspiration** until the implementation establishes its gameplay trigger.

- **0.0–1.8 s:** black/gray intro backdrop; angular white interlocking-star-like logo; `Larp Client` text; `Press to enter` prompt, subtle entrance and sparkle treatment. Recreate the overall hierarchy, *not* the logo or brand.
- **2.0–3.8 s:** title menu appears with small vertically stacked options including Singleplayer, Multiplayer, Minecraft Settings, Client, Quit Game. Tall, soft, wavy arcs and small star ornaments on right; character/skin preview briefly appears. Main menu is sparse rather than crowded.

- **6.0–8.5 s:** backdrop shifts from vivid fiery red/orange sky into purple storm/cosmic rift. Bright purple line/trail and geometric wire-frame shapes. Character continues moving; trail and environmental background are dominant visuals.

Top compact black status module includes icons/name/domain/FPS/time; target NPC name `TestNPC_91` plus apparent health, range and ping readout. Glowing circles at ground level and particle glints. Treat target distance/health overlays as **private testing only** if a public server forbids them.
- **11.0–14.7 s:** a large stylized humanoid model next to NPCs, bright floor circle, sparkling fragments and a dark figure lying or posed horizontally; likely a showcase of custom models/animations rather than proof of live player-death replacement.

## Fidelity priorities

1. Character-centered **dramatic celestial cosmetics**: glow, wings, trails, orbiting glyphs, clear palette variations.
2. Smooth **black/gray minimalist main menu** with soft decorative arcs, crisp typography, custom avatar preview, focus/hover movement.
3. Client menu + search/categories + preview/enable/disable, extensive module customization.
4. PvP feedback: target HUD, hit effects, kill/victory effects **where reliable and permitted**.
5. Beautiful custom sky/background presentations, with multiplayer-safe visibility and performance toggles.

## Do not promise

- Other players will see local-only visual effects without a compatible server/plugin/client.
- Any animation shown in video automatically indicates an in-game kill.
- The extraordinarily high FPS number briefly visible in a clip will be achievable on any machine or with visual effects enabled.
- Proprietary Larp Client models/logo/effects can be copied verbatim.
